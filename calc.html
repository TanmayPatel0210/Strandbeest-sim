Birth Of The Beest

In early 1990s Jansen was working on a mechanism that could mimic the movement of an animal limb walking. He had an algorithm that could eventually find the way.

The Problem

He didn't have a supercomputer to run the simulations. He used a classic Atari computer which calculated thousands of random leg proportions...

The Survival of Numbers

The computer simulated them one after another, and started killing the ones who couldn't walk. It bred together the best ones together for months around the clock without break

The HOLY Numbers

After months of work and millions of permutations and combinations the computer spit out 13 perfect leg proportions which eventually gave birth to the StrandBeest
